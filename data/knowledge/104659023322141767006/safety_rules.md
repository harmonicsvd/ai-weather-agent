# Safety / PPE notes (working copy, not official policy doc)

user_sub: 104659023322141767006
risk preference: low
PPE needed on all site visits

minimum kit:
- helmet
- hi-vis vest
- steel-toe shoes
- gloves

weather add-ons:
- rain shell + anti-slip cover if rain likely
- thermal layer if colder morning (esp. long outdoor checks)
- eye protection when windy/dusty near loading areas

rough thresholds i follow:
- wind >= 30 km/h -> no roof/elevated work unless site lead confirms safe
- wind >= 40 km/h -> postpone non-critical outdoor checks
- heavy rain / storm warning -> switch to indoor scope or move meeting
- evening low visibility -> reflective vest + portable light required

movement reminders:
- wet ramps/stairs: slow down, keep hand support if available
- stick to marked walk paths in yard/port
- no restricted zone access without local escort
- if thunder warning starts, pause outdoor inspection

"safety first > schedule. if in doubt, reduce scope and document why."
